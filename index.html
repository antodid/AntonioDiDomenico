Research

Do Chinese Capital Controls Raise the Cost of Debt for European Firms?

Bachelor Thesis · Utrecht University · Graded 9.2 / 10

I wanted to know whether the restrictions Beijing places on cross-border capital flows show up in what European companies pay to borrow. To find out I used twenty-two years of data on the 600 companies in the STOXX Europe 600, and most of my time went into cleaning and matching that data long before I ran anything. It’s the grade I’m proudest of, mainly because the question was mine.

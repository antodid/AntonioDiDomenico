About

I'm Antonio, an Italian student who came to the Netherlands at eighteen to study economics without ever having taken an economics class before. That gap turned out to be the interesting part — I had to build the foundation from scratch, and I found I liked it.

Since then I've followed my curiosity more than any plan. I spent a semester in Rio de Janeiro, learned Portuguese in bars and on the back of motorbikes rather than in classrooms, and came back more comfortable with not knowing what comes next. I've built a map app, run a student newsletter, tutored statistics, and organised a food drive — not because they fit a strategy, but because each one let me make or fix something real.

This September I start an MSc in Finance at RSM Rotterdam. What connects everything I do is the same instinct: I learn fastest when I'm in the middle of something I don't yet know how to finish.

Research

Do Chinese Capital Controls Raise the Cost of Debt for European Firms?

Bachelor Thesis · Utrecht University · Graded 9.2 / 10

My thesis examines whether Chinese capital controls — the restrictions Beijing places on cross-border capital flows — make it more expensive for European companies to borrow. I tested this on panel data of 600 STOXX Europe 600 firms across 22 years. The result was a 9.2 — the grade I'm proudest of, not for the number but because the question was mine.

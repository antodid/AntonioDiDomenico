Research

Do Chinese Capital Controls Raise the Cost of Debt for European Firms?

Bachelor Thesis · Utrecht University · Graded 9.2 / 10

My thesis examines whether Chinese capital controls — the restrictions Beijing places on cross-border capital flows — make it more expensive for European companies to borrow. I tested this on panel data of 600 STOXX Europe 600 firms across 22 years. The result was a 9.2 — the grade I'm proudest of, not for the number but because the question was mine.
